# Retention and quality gate

1. Retrieve what was actually taught; do NOT show the answer before recall.
2. Select a different but equivalent prompt, with matching difficulty and declared scope.
3. Assess domain-specific correctness, explanation, transfer, assistance, calibration.
4. Correct errors; record the observed answer and evidence of feedback.
5. Update the due date adaptively. Initial intervals (1, 3, 7, 21 days) are a starting heuristic, not magic biological constants.
6. Review does not block moving on to the next unit unless the skill is a strict prerequisite or safety-critical competency.
7. Stale/due evidence degrades claims from `maintained` to `retention_due` until retested. Never infer durable proficiency from feeling familiar.
8. The review log must distinguish an AI-generated question from an answered question; invented attempts and dates are prohibited.
